Title: SafeBase Now Unified Under Drata

URL Source: https://help.safebase.io/en/articles/16203200-safebase-now-unified-under-drata

Markdown Content:
The SafeBase brand is now unified with Drata. This update reflects the continued evolution of the Drata Agentic Trust Management Platform and brings governance, risk, compliance, and assurance capabilities together under a single brand.

This article explains what is changing and what it means for customers.

## **What’s changing**

Drata is consolidating the SafeBase brand into the Drata brand.

As part of this update:

## **What’s not changing**

This update is a **brand change only**. There are no changes to the products, services, or platform experience customers rely on today.

This transition represents **continued investment in the platform**, reflecting the unified trust management experience Drata is building.

## **Do customers need to take any action?**

No action is required. Your current setup will continue to work as expected, including:

If any updates are required in the future, we will communicate them in advance.

## **Frequently Asked Questions**

## **What exactly is changing? Is SafeBase going away?**

The SafeBase brand is being unified under Drata to create a single brand delivering the **Drata Agentic Trust Management Platform**.

## **Will SafeBase features still exist?**

Yes. This update does **not remove existing features or capabilities**.

Product enhancements will continue as part of Drata’s ongoing platform development.

## **What happens to my SafeBase login?**

Your login and product access remain available at **app.safebase.io**.

If future updates change access points, customers will receive advance notice.

## **What happens to the SafeBase Help Center?**

The Help Center remains accessible at **[help.safebase.io](https://help.safebase.io/)**. Over time, articles will be updated to reflect Drata branding and product naming.

## **Who is my account owner now—SafeBase or Drata?**

Customers will continue working with the **same teams they work with today**.

## **Does this change how data is stored or handled?**

No. This brand transition does **not change how customer data is stored or handled.**SafeBase data continues to be hosted in the **North American region**.

## **Are there new compliance implications?**

No. This brand update **does not introduce new compliance requirements**.
